# Outdoor HomeOS card artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Generic illustrations identify equipment categories; they do not assert an installed brand, model, certification, or physical location. All final assets are transparent PNGs in `assets/homeos/outdoor/`. Existing hose-bibb, main shutoff valve, and whole-home filter illustrations are reused where appropriate.

## Shared style

Use case: product-mockup. One HomeOS equipment card illustration per image. Realistic premium studio product cutout, physically plausible hardware, precise material textures, soft neutral upper-left lighting. Entire object centered at a three-quarter angle with margin, square composition, clear at card size. Genuine transparent alpha background; no ground plane, colored backdrop, frame, logo, watermark, or unrelated objects.

## Review

All twelve final assets were visually inspected. Earlier backflow and cleanout variants were discarded; only the corrected final PNGs are bundled. Semantic matching distinguishes a hose-connection vacuum breaker from the combined hose bibb and from an irrigation backflow assembly. Future photos from the company can identify specific valve models.
